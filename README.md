# Briefly summarize The Gaming Room client and their software requirements. Who was the client? What type of software did they want you to design?

With htis application, they wanted my team and I to write up our reccomendations on the various ways to ensure their application would be able to handle the expanded user interactions.

# What about the process of working through a design document did you find helpful when developing the code?
  Taking the time to write out a design document was helpful, beacuase it allows you to really think about the program in front of you and as you consider the different aspects of the program. It gives you a map of what the program should be in the end. It is like writing out pseudocode, it gives you a reference of what you are attempting to build, so that you are not getting stuck on what to do next.

# How did you interpret the user’s needs and implement them into your software design? Why is it so important to consider the user’s needs when designing?
  To interpret the user's needs, I read over the documentation from The Gaming Room and what they wanted their application to be and from there, I put myself into the shoes of someone who would play this game and imagine what kind of features I would like from it. ALong with how I think it should run and operate to make my time as a user the most enjoyable. I think when you are developing an application, it is important to consider the user's and thier needs, because they will be the ones largely using the application and will determine the overall success of it.

# How did you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a similar software application?
  My biggest approach for designing this application, was to take into consideration what the client wanted to get from their application and with the thought of the users in mind. I think this is an effective approach, beacuase at the end of the day, you are being employed by the client to build the program and should try to adhere to their wishes whenever possible, but to take in to account the users and their needs, because the client may not always know what will be the best options for this.
